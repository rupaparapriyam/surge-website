01 / 07 • SEA SALT SPRAY

Flat, limp hair with zero texture.

What's in it: Real sea salt for grip. Aloe and glycerin so it doesn't dry your hair out like the cheap stuff does.

What it does: Undone, beachy texture. Second-day hair, on day one.

02 / 07 • TEXTURE POWDER

Flat roots, no grip, hair that won't hold a style.

What's in it: A starch that soaks up oil at the root. No white cast, no chalky residue.

What it does: Instant root lift. Matte finish. Grip without the crunch.

03 / 07 • POMADE

Needing real hold without looking like you dumped oil on your head.

What's in it: Natural waxes. Not the greasy stuff from your dad's bathroom cabinet.

What it does: Slicked or texturized — your call. Hold that lasts all day, shine that doesn't look fake.

04 / 07 • CLAY

Frizz and flyaways, wanting a strong hold with zero shine.

What's in it: Clay that pulls oil out as the day goes on, keeping you matte instead of greasy by 3pm.

What it does: Strong hold. Zero shine. Texture that holds up all day.

05 / 07 • HAIR STYLING MOUSSE

Fine or thin hair that goes flat by noon.

What's in it: A light foam that won't leave your hair stiff, sticky, or flaky.

What it does: Volume that actually moves. Not stiff, not crunchy, not fake.

06 / 07 • LEAVE-IN CONDITIONER

Dry, frizzy, hard-to-manage hair between washes.

What's in it: A moisture and protein mix. No rinsing, no waiting around.

What it does: Soft, easy, lightweight hair.

07 / 07 • HAIR OIL

Dryness, split ends, dull-looking hair.

What's in it: A light oil blend that soaks in fast — no greasy residue.

What it does: Shine and control, without the slicked-back grease look.
